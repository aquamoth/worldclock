Privacy policy

World Clock, by Trustfall AB · Updated 19 August 2026

Who we are

World Clock is made by Trustfall AB, Sweden. For anything about the app or this policy, write to support@trustfall.se.

What the app collects

Nothing. World Clock has no accounts, no ads, and no trackers. Your clock collection and settings are stored only on your device. The app sends nothing to us or to anyone else, so there is nothing for us to secure, retain, or delete.

What “no tracking” means to us

We never collect or sell personal data or user behavior. A future version may add anonymous, aggregate counters — for example how many times the app has been installed or started — so we can tell whether it is being used. If that happens, this policy will say so before it ships, and it will never identify you.
